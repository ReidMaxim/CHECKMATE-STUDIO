## Usage

- Opening A removes the old public wordmark. The archived exact master is also included. Opening B uses the unobtrusive text mark. Opening C adds a small crowned fish in the same space. B is selected for this release because the opening question already has a job to do.
- On a 1080 × 1920 master, place the opening signature at x=92, y=147, with a maximum footprint of 205 × 42 pixels. Share the opening with the hook. Keep the board, move indicators, captions and tactical squares clear.
- After the final caption, the chosen cut places the small wordmark and optional tagline below the board. The result card and the recorded board position remain visible. Channel banners, covers and profiles are the mascot's main home.
- Keep the joke in the brand. George stays measured and informative. A lightly cheeky cover or pinned comment works when it follows the chess. Skip jokes that make a historical result, a teaching point or a player's intentions sound invented.
- Keep the wordmark, tagline and avatar inside the banner's central band for cropped views. The covers keep essential text inside the central vertical panel. Check the mobile upload crop before using it on a channel.
