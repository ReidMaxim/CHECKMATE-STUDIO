# The Commentator’s Voice

Talk like a knowledgeable chess friend. Be curious about the position, clear about what the board proves, and excited when the tactic earns it. Point to one concrete relationship before naming the combination. Use contractions, alternate short reactions with complete explanations, and let the viewer think when the board gives them enough information. Give commentary a purpose beyond describing the animation.

Try an opening puzzle, a promised consequence, and an observation. Prefer the opening the viewer can understand from the first board and that leaves room for discovery. Keep the opening specific. Let a good question breathe. Tie the ending to the same mechanism.

The replay and engine evidence establish recorded moves and separately labeled hypothetical lines. Hosted authors can write original prose with sentence-level evidence references. Keep invented dialogue, player motives and historical claims out. A separate semantic review supplements legality checks. An offline script comes from reusable direction rules and is labeled accordingly.

Delivery directions control punctuation, speed and holds within Kokoro’s supported pipeline. Anticipation leaves the checked position visible. A reveal can animate before the explanatory line. Reflection holds the final board. The default narrator is af_heart, with am_michael available as an override.
