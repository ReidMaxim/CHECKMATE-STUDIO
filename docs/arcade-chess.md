# ARCADE//CHESS prototype

An experimental 26-second vertical cut on the dedicated `mission-002-a-arcade-chess` branch, built from the Mission 002 evidence. The source is the published Légal continuation used in the Director's Cut. Both show the same starting position and recorded five half-moves: Nxe5, Bxd1, Bxf7+, Ke7, Nd5#. The documentary benchmark is the previously approved 38.3-second Kokoro cut.

The arcade composition has six original hand-drawn pixel silhouettes, a position-driven HUD, short move hops and three burst cues (capture, check, mate). The announcer is Kokoro 82M v1.0 INT8, `am_puck` at 1.06×, using the existing CPU provider and checksummed model. The bass loop and effects are generated from original synthesized tones. Voice, effects and capture timing come from the arcade cue file.

The prototype uses the existing Remotion renderer through a dedicated composition entry. The branch-only cloud workflow validates the Mission 002 evidence and the documentary position match, generates the audio, renders the 1080 × 1920 H.264/AAC cut and verifies its decode and audio headroom. Open Artifacts for the MP4 and the cue/evidence audit.

For an A/B viewing, compare clarity of the exposed queen and forcing reply, how the capture and mate land, and whether the voice adds character at phone volume. The two cuts have different runtimes, narration, sound design and visual pacing. Treat the comparison as a preference test between complete cuts.
