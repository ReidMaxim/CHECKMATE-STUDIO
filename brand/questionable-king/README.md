# STOCKFOOL · Logo review

Three original vector directions share the STOCKFOOL name, tagline and green, gold, ivory and forest palette. The isolated marks and horizontal lockups have transparent backgrounds. The 800 × 800 profile previews have square backgrounds with the marks centered for a circular crop. Each 2560 × 1440 sample banner keeps the lockup in the central band.

| Direction | What to look for at 48 px |
| --- | --- |
| **01 · Confused King** | The wide cross and stepped plinth identify the king. The two tilted punctuation marks give this version its attitude. They are the smallest details in the set. |
| **02 · King of Questions** | The single large question mark reads first. The three-point crown carries the royal reference. |
| **03 · Questionable King** | The king remains readable as a silhouette. The question mark is cut out of the torso and takes on the color behind it. |

Run `scripts/export-questionable-king.sh` with Node, Inkscape and ImageMagick to export the SVGs, PNGs and native-size checks. Compare the three concepts before choosing. These are review assets, with film integration reserved for the approved design.
